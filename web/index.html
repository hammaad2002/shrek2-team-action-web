Shrek 2 Team Action

Play your own copy of the 2004 PC game in this browser. The game files come from your disc image and stay on this device.

Works in current desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, in a normal (not private) window. Needs about 1 GB of storage for this site. Unpacking the disc takes about a minute in Chrome and Safari and three to four minutes in Firefox, once.

Checking this browser…

Choose your Shrek 2 Team Action disc image (.iso). It is unpacked into this browser's private storage (about 900 MB).

Saved games

Your progress is kept only in this browser. Back it up to keep it safe or to carry it to another browser or computer. Restoring also accepts the save files of a Windows installation (save.bin, save.bin_2 and save.bin_3 from the game's Save folder).

Settings

These are the options of the game's original Launcher. Changes are saved as you make them and apply the next time you press Play.
